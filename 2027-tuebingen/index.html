Symposium VII · Forthcoming

The Seventh Symposium

Wednesday 17 – Friday 19 March 2027

University of Tübingen

Overview Call for papers Important dates Programme Venue & travel Registration Contact Organizers

Overview

The seventh meeting of the series will take place at the University of Tübingen, the first to be held in Germany.

The programme is by invitation. Attendance is free, and the meeting will also be streamed online. To take part, email the organizers.
